# Naming Review

## Preliminary finding

An initial public web search found no obvious exact use of **“S.P.I.N. Engineering Communication Standard.”** It did find unrelated uses of “SPIN” in sales communication, strategic communication, influencing frameworks, and software process improvement networks.

This is not a trademark clearance search and does not establish availability in any jurisdiction or class.

## Publication caution

Before public launch:

- search relevant trademark registers and software/documentation marketplaces;
- review possible confusion with established SPIN selling and communication frameworks;
- confirm the chosen GitHub organisation, domain, and social handles;
- avoid claiming exclusive ownership of the acronym; and
- obtain qualified legal advice if the project will be formally branded, promoted, or registered.
