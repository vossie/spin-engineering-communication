# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while remaining precise about impact.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and security boundaries.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of sensitive information, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using the contact method published in the repository profile. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Publication should not proceed until a monitored private contact address is configured.
