# Contributing

Thank you for helping make S.P.I.N. clearer and easier to adopt.

## Good contributions

- clearer wording without changing the four-field meaning;
- realistic fictional examples;
- accessibility improvements;
- integrations and templates for common engineering tools;
- spelling, grammar, and broken-link fixes; and
- translations that retain Subject and Priority as the only mandatory fields.

## Before opening a change

1. Keep all examples fictional and public-safe.
2. Do not add mandatory fields.
3. Keep ownership inside Subject.
4. Preserve the precedence of incident and security policies.
5. Run `python scripts/validate.py` and `mkdocs build --strict`.
6. Explain the communication problem your change addresses.
