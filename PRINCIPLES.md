# Design Principles

S.P.I.N. is governed by eight principles:

1. **Simplicity over bureaucracy.** A routine report should take less than one minute.
2. **Clear communication over unnecessary detail.** State what the recipient needs to understand and act.
3. **Evidence over assumptions.** Separate observed facts, hypotheses, and unknowns.
4. **Appropriate urgency over emotional escalation.** Priority follows impact and response need.
5. **Technical ownership over organisational politics.** Route work without using ownership as blame.
6. **Early reporting over delayed certainty.** Report material concerns early and label uncertainty.
7. **Engineer-to-engineer collaboration.** Optimise messages for shared diagnosis and resolution.
8. **Compatibility with existing workflows.** Complement the tools and policies already in place.

The core principle is:

> Raise issues early. Communicate clearly. Escalate appropriately.
