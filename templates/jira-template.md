# Jira Template

Use the S.P.I.N. message at the top of Description. Jira's native assignee, component, severity, and workflow fields remain tool metadata rather than new S.P.I.N. fields.

```text
Subject: @Team | Brief description

Priority: Normal | Non-production | Business Hours

Impact: [Optional] What is affected?

Note: [Optional] Supporting evidence or dependency.
```

Detailed reproduction steps and acceptance criteria may follow below the message according to local Jira practice.
